Santali lessons are written in Bengali script, but the spoken language is Santali. A Bengali voice is not a Santali voice and may mispronounce the lesson. Bhasha Setu uses approved Santali recordings when every section has one; otherwise text playback needs a Santali voice. AI drafts are labeled and should be checked by a teacher.

Generate and review Santali audio

For an approved lesson, one reviewer can generate a speech draft or add a human reading, listen to the clip, and approve it for student lessons.

1 - Approve the lesson text

One teacher or community expert reviews the translation.

2 - Generate Santali audio

Generate an AI speech draft, or add a human reading.

3 - Listen and approve

One reviewer listens to the full recording and approves it before students can play it.

Browser microphone access requires HTTPS or localhost. On school Wi-Fi without HTTPS, record with the device's recorder and upload the audio file. Supported formats: WebM, Ogg, WAV, MP4, and MP3. Santali recordings stay on this school server; ask the speaker before recording.

Speech generation uses a separate Santali TTS model. First use needs optional speech dependencies, Hugging Face model access, and internet to download several GB of model weights. CPU-only computers may take several minutes and need substantial memory. Santali pronunciation has not been independently evaluated; every generated clip stays a draft until a fluent reviewer checks it.
